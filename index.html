Echo Squad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Echo Squad does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your progress (runs, settings and stats) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. The game does not connect to the internet.

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first.
